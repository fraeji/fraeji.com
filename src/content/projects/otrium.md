During my time at Otrium, I produced editorial shoots and wrote engaging copy while keeping the look and feel consistent with the brand’s identity and vision.

## Otrium x You with Winter van den Brink

At Otrium, we encouraged shoppers to create their own lookbooks and personal style rather than chasing trends. Working with esteemed fashion photographer Winter van den Brink, I created lookbooks with five models that emphasised their personal tastes, personalities and individualities. We shot these visuals around Amsterdam and created short-form video interviews to accompany each subject. I led the art direction and storyline for this project, working directly with Winter and our video team.

## Festive campaign FW21

_Do it twice as good_

For our 2021 festive campaign, we embraced the theme of opulence within everyday life after a difficult two years of staying indoors and distancing from loved ones. The result of this was to go ‘all out’ – think glittery extravagance mingling with otherwise mundane, everyday themes of the city.

With the usual theme of festivity and celebration fully embraced, we wanted to encourage our customers to think about living in the moment and enjoying the time they spend with others. After a long time of being isolated, it’s time to push the boat out with your nearest and dearest, dressed up and over the top to make up for lost time.

## Brand-specific: Alexander McQueen

For our brand-specific project for legendary designer Alexander McQueen, respecting the style of the clothes and the brand’s legacy came first and foremost.

We wanted the softness of the warm cedar wood to contrast against the rougher leather and acrylic pieces that the collection featured. Steely poses and eye contact from the models gave it the edge and attention we needed.
