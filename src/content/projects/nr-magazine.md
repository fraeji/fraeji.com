Founded in London in 2016, NR Magazine is an independent publication exploring culture at large, with a focus on art, design, film and music. For their 17th edition, I proofed and copy-edited all articles, featuring artists like VTSS, Ellen Allien, Santiago Sierra and Jon Rafman.

Additionally, I conducted an interview with Primavera Festival curator Joan Pons about the 2023 festival, discussing social progression within the festival and the connections between Barcelona, Madrid and the music festival industry.
